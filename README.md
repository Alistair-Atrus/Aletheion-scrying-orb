# Aletheion — Scrying Orb

A scrying orb kept warm by a fire in the Forest of Plastic Trees.

Open `index.html` in a browser, or visit the GitHub Pages site for this repo.

## What it does

- **Scry** — click the orb or press Scry for one glyph from the Glyphic Cybermancy map, with its phonetic, word, and a short reading.
- **Ask a question** — type a question first; it is shown with the reading and kept only in your browser.
- **Draw three glyphs** — a three-glyph spread: Assertion → Transformation → Anchoring.
- **Light the campfire** — warms the orb and lights the fire in the forest.
- **Copy reading** — copies the latest reading to the clipboard.
- **Reading history** — past readings are saved in the browser (localStorage, up to 30), with a Clear history button.
- **Today's glyph** — one deterministic glyph per local date.
- **Today's spread** — three deterministic glyphs for the local date: Assertion → Transformation → Anchoring.
- **Browse the lexicon** — searchable list of all 138 glyphs with phonetic and definition. Click any entry to consult that glyph as a single scry.
- **Copy history** — copies all saved readings as text, alongside the Clear history button.

Single file, no tracking, no server, no external dependencies. Works offline.

## Glyphs

Glyphs are drawn from the Glyphic Cybermancy map. The readings on the page are prompts for reflection, not predictions.
